PF withdrawal after leaving a job: common questions

When can I withdraw my PF after leaving a job?

For an online Form 19 (PF final settlement), the EPFO online claim FAQ says you should not submit the claim before two months have passed since you left the establishment, and you must not currently be working in a PF-covered establishment. If you are joining another PF-covered job soon, EPFO suggests transferring the account instead of withdrawing.

What do I need before filing Form 19 online on the EPFO portal?

Per the EPFO online claim FAQ: an activated UAN, Aadhaar seeded and verified with OTP eKYC, your bank account and IFSC seeded against the UAN, and PAN seeded if your service is under five years. Your date of joining and date of exit must also be recorded in the EPFO database.

What is Form 10C and who can claim it online?

Form 10C is the EPS (pension) claim form, used either for a withdrawal benefit or for a Scheme Certificate that keeps your pension membership. Per the same EPFO FAQ, the online withdrawal benefit needs total service of more than 6 months and under 9.5 years.

Should I transfer or withdraw my PF when I change jobs?

If you are joining another PF-covered job, EPFO's Which Claim Form page notes you can get the account transferred to the new employer rather than withdrawing. Withdrawal through Form 19 and Form 10C is for when you have left PF-covered employment. Check the official page and your own situation before deciding.

What is the Composite Claim Form (Aadhaar)?

It is a single EPFO claim form that, per EPFO's composite claim form circular, can be used in place of separate Forms 19, 10C and 31 when your Aadhaar and bank details are seeded against your UAN.

What should I check before my PF claim so it is not rejected?

The online claim depends on the prerequisites in the EPFO FAQ being met: an activated UAN, Aadhaar, bank and (where needed) PAN seeded, and a date of exit on record. The free checklist on this page walks through those items so you can catch them before you submit. The rejection reason shown on the EPFO portal for your own claim is the one to act on.
