Not EPFO, labour or tax advice. Rules and portals change. Always confirm on the official EPFO pages linked below and with your employer or a qualified professional before you file or pay anything. Prefer transfer to a new employer if you are joining another PF-covered job soon.

Why this exists

  • EPFO online claim FAQ says Form 19 / Form 10C need an activated UAN, Aadhaar seeded with OTP eKYC, and bank + IFSC seeded; PAN is needed for Form 19 when service is under 5 years.
  • Form 19 online also needs joining & exit dates in the EPFO database, you must not be currently under a PF-covered establishment, and you must wait at least two months after exit.
  • Form 10C withdrawal benefit online additionally needs total service more than 6 months and under 9.5 years (per the same FAQ). Many people only discover missing KYC after a rejected claim.

Official sources: EPFO Member Interface · Online Claim FAQ (PDF) · Which Claim Form.

3. Transfer vs withdraw (plain prompts)

  • Joining another PF job soon? EPFO Which Claim Form page notes you can wait and get the account transferred instead of withdrawing.
  • Form 19: PF final settlement after exit (online path subject to KYC + timing rules above).
  • Form 10C: EPS Withdrawal Benefit or Scheme Certificate - retaining membership can help toward future pension eligibility (see official page notes).
  • Composite Claim Form (Aadhaar): may replace separate 19 / 10C / 31 when Aadhaar + bank are seeded (per EPFO composite-form circular).
